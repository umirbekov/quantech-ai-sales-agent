# Quantech — AI Sales Agent

> AI-powered B2B sales automation platform that qualifies leads, engages customers 24/7, automates follow-ups, and helps businesses convert more opportunities into customers.

## 🎯 Problem

Businesses lose potential customers because sales teams cannot respond instantly, manually qualify every lead, consistently follow up, and manage growing customer conversations at scale.

## 💡 Solution

Quantech provides an AI Sales Agent that communicates with prospects 24/7, qualifies and scores leads, automates follow-ups, supports sales workflows, and helps managers focus on high-value opportunities.

## 🚀 Key Features

- 24/7 AI Sales Agent
- Automated Lead Qualification
- Lead Scoring
- Intelligent Follow-up Automation
- CRM Workflow Integration
- Manager Alerts
- AI Recommendations
- Sales Analytics & Reports
- Multi-Manager Support

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. A potential customer enters the sales funnel.
2. Quantech AI engages and qualifies the lead.
3. The system analyzes lead intent and priority.
4. Qualified opportunities are routed into the sales workflow.
5. Follow-ups and manager notifications are automated.
6. Sales teams focus on the highest-value opportunities.

## 🧠 Technology

Quantech is designed around AI-driven sales automation, natural language processing, intelligent workflow automation, analytics, and cloud-based infrastructure.

## 💼 Business Model

Quantech operates as a B2B SaaS platform with subscription-based plans and additional enterprise solutions for businesses requiring advanced workflows, integrations, and multi-branch deployment.
